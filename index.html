About ABHA

ABHA is an Aatmanirbhar Bharat initiative founded by Rahul Amrutkar in 2020,inspired by the need for social reforms during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Focused on fostering social happiness through rural Entrepreneurship and village transformation.

Core initiatives

  1. (Indian Industrialists and Technocrafts Estates):-scale industrial estates designed for roadside villages.Focus on 5-acre clusters for efficient and sustainable growth.Promotes MSME industries in a decentratized,collaborative model.

  2. Atal Gotha Factory: Implements circular economy principles leveraging village-specific resources.A holistic village transformation model combining technology with social intent.

  3. Balance Sheets:A unique approach to evaluate and benchmark the potential for rural transformation.
